## Как делать
1. **Фото:** Gemini или Google Flow (labs.google/flow), формат 9:16, 3–4 варианта — выбери один.
2. **Оживить:** Flow → «Кадры в видео»: первый кадр = последний кадр = это фото, 8 секунд.
3. Ролик — Z.ai (часть Г задания): склеит петлю, зальёт, отметит «залит». В приложение ставит Claude.

## Тест 1 — простой: «Американский психопат»
Белая стерильная квартира 1987 года ночью: прозрачный дождевик на вешалке, газеты на полу, топор у дивана, стерео, окно на Манхэттен.

Фото:
```
A minimalist white Manhattan luxury apartment in 1987 at night, empty. In the foreground, sharp: a transparent plastic raincoat hanging on a chrome coat stand, the white floor around it neatly covered with spread newspapers with blurry unreadable print. Beside it: a fire axe with a red head leaning against a white leather sofa and a silver hi-fi stereo with glowing green level meters. Behind, out of focus: a big window with rain on the glass and the Manhattan skyline at night. Light: cold white ceiling light and the blue night city. Mood: sterile white perfection with something horribly wrong underneath. Vertical 9:16. A real 35mm film photograph: natural light, soft film grain, real materials with small imperfections, real-world sizes; not CGI, not a 3D render, not glossy. Shallow depth of field: the foreground is sharp, the background is softly out of focus. No people, no hands, no faces, no text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.
```
Оживить:
```
Animate this exact image for 8 seconds. The camera is locked off: no zoom, no pan, no cuts. Only these things move, gently and continuously the whole time: the green level meters on the stereo bounce softly to music; raindrops run slowly down the big window; the city lights twinkle behind the glass. The raincoat, the newspapers and the axe do not move. Everything else stays perfectly still. The light does not change. Nothing new appears and nothing disappears; no people. The last frame is identical to the first frame, so the video loops seamlessly. No smoke, fog or mist.
```

## Тест 2 — сложный: «Волк с Уолл-стрит»
Торговый зал брокерской фирмы ночью после вечеринки: рыбка в аквариуме, шампанское, снятая трубка, модель суперкара; за ними зелёные котировки.

Фото:
```
The trading floor of a 1990s Long Island brokerage firm at night, the morning after a wild office party, nobody left. In the foreground, sharp, on the nearest desk: a round glass fishbowl with one orange goldfish, a half-full champagne glass, a beige office telephone with the handset off the hook and a small white scale model of a wedge-shaped 1980s Italian supercar. Behind, out of focus: long rows of desks with boxy beige computer monitors showing small glowing green digits, a long ticker band glowing green across the far wall, confetti on the carpet. Light: one warm brass desk lamp and the cold green glow of the screens. Mood: gold and money-green; greed and excess after the party. Vertical 9:16. A real 35mm film photograph: natural light, soft film grain, real materials with small imperfections, real-world sizes; not CGI, not a 3D render, not glossy. Shallow depth of field: the foreground is sharp, the background is softly out of focus. No people, no hands, no faces, no text or logos; only small blurry green digits on the screens.
```
Оживить:
```
Animate this exact image for 8 seconds. The camera is locked off: no zoom, no pan, no cuts. Only these things move, gently and continuously the whole time: the goldfish hovers in place waving its fins and slowly turns around in the bowl; bubbles rise in the champagne glass; the small green digits on the screens flicker; the ticker band on the far wall scrolls slowly from right to left. Everything else stays perfectly still. The light does not change. Nothing new appears and nothing disappears; no people. The last frame is identical to the first frame, so the video loops seamlessly. No smoke, fog or mist.
```
