Four things everyone believes about HIV. And one thing almost nobody knows.
One. Mosquitoes can spread it.
False. A mosquito doesn't inject the last person's blood into you, and HIV can't multiply inside it.
Two. You can catch it from a kiss, a hug, or a toilet seat.
False. Saliva, sweat and tears don't pass HIV. Neither do shared cups.
Three. You can tell by looking.
False. People can feel fine for years. More than one in ten people with HIV don't know they have it.
Four. It's a death sentence.
False, for decades now. With treatment started early, people with HIV can live about as long as everyone else.
On treatment, the virus can drop so low that it can't be passed on through sex. Doctors call it U equals U.
Now the thing almost nobody knows.
If you think you were exposed, there are emergency pills called PEP.
They work if you start within 72 hours. The sooner, the better.
Save this. One day, you or a friend might need it.
